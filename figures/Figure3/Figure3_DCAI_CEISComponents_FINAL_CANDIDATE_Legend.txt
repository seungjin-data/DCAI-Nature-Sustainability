Figure 3 | CEIS component prevalence among 25 confirmed-tier countries.
Horizontal bars show the proportion of confirmed-tier countries that have adopted each CEIS component. Components are grouped by CEIS function: CEI-1, CEI-2, and CEI-4 represent strategic alignment and direct climate-policy linkage; CEI-3 and CEI-5 represent implementation and coordination mechanisms; CEI-6 represents reporting visibility. Numeric order is not used; components are arranged to reflect their functional layer within the CEIS framework. Hollow circle markers on CEI-3 and CEI-5 indicate sensitivity values excluding CHN and ARE. Full country-level values are provided in Source Data and Supplementary Table S4.
