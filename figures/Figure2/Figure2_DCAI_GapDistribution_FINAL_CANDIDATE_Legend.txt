Figure 2 | Checkpoint accounting and extension of positive Digital–Climate Alignment Gaps across plotted countries.

Panel A shows the 75-country checkpoint structure, comprising 71 plotted rows and four data-pending countries (EGY, RWA, ROU, BGR). The stacked bar represents 25 confirmed-tier countries (filled blue), 46 provisional plotted countries (medium grey), and 4 data-pending countries not plotted (beige hatched). Panel B shows a two-group strip plot of ACI–CEIS gaps for 71 plotted country rows. Values greater than zero indicate that AI Capacity Institutionalization exceeds Climate–Energy Integration Score. Filled blue points indicate confirmed-tier countries and open grey points indicate provisional plotted countries. The orange dashed vertical line indicates the confirmed-tier mean gap of 58.3 percentage points. Full country-level values are provided in Source Data and Supplementary Table S4.
